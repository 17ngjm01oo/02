About & Sources

About

This website provides country-level data, rankings, and comparisons across major international indicators, making global statistics easier to search, compare, and explore.

Linking to This Website

Links to any page are welcome without prior permission. When sharing data or charts, please link to the relevant page so readers can verify the source and latest values.

Estimates and Projections

Some data may include estimates or projections depending on the source, location, and year. See the original source for methodology details.

Regional Classification

Regional groupings follow the UN M49 standard. For browsing purposes, Central America and the Caribbean are included under North America.

Country and Territory Coverage

Coverage is based on the UN M49 “countries or areas” standard. Entries are displayed as countries or territories for navigation purposes only and do not imply any position on sovereignty or political status.

Flag Icons

Flag icons are used for identification. Territories may be shown with an administering country’s flag or a local flag, depending on official status and common usage. Their use does not imply any position on sovereignty, recognition, or political status.

Rounding of Figures

Figures may be rounded for readability. Rankings may use underlying source data rather than the displayed rounded values.

Euro Area Local Currency Data

For countries that adopted the euro, pre-adoption local-currency data may be shown in euros using official fixed conversion rates. See the IMF World Economic Outlook FAQ for details.
